# Ine Røyert – Copy & Strategy Guide

Oct 15, 2026 · @AweSM - 2

Ine sells capacity, not motivation: mental training for ambitious women founders who have become their own bottleneck, with KAPASITETSKODEN™ (14.900 kr) as the flagship. Every piece of copy should move a reader one step up that ladder, in Ine's warm, direct, story-led Norwegian.

## Positioning and audience

Ine has narrowed from broad motivation coaching (2021–22) to one sharp promise in 2026: build the mental capacity to carry the growth you want, without paying for it with health, energy or family.

**Core promise (her words):** «Kapasitetskoden™ hjelper kvinnelige gründere som har blitt sin egen flaskehals å gå fra tankekjør, stress og overlevelsesmodus til mer kapasitet, klarhet og gjennomføring.»

**One-line positioning to reuse:** Mentaltrening for ambisiøse kvinner som vil vokse videre – uten å bruke seg selv opp.

**Primary reader:** a Norwegian woman, roughly 30–50, who runs her own business or carries a lot of leadership responsibility, has a family, and is a self-described high achiever. She has already read the books and listened to the podcasts.

**Secondary readers:** leaders and teams (workshops and talks), book buyers, and women in a life transition (the 2024 «alt raknet» readers).

| What she feels now | What she wants |
| --- | --- |
| Hodet logger aldri helt av, også når hun har fri | Være ordentlig til stede, på jobb og hjemme |
| Tankekjør og overtenking rundt beslutninger | Ta beslutninger raskere og stole på seg selv |
| Behov for kontroll; ro bare når alt går etter planen | Stå stødig i usikkerhet |
| Sitter alene med alt ansvaret | En sparringspartner og et fellesskap |
| Holder igjen på vekst fordi «mer» betyr mer press | Vekst som ikke koster mer av henne |
| Flytter målstreken før hun har feiret | Kjenne at hun faktisk har kommet et stykke |

**Objections to answer in every launch:**

- «Dette klarer jeg selv.» → Innsikt er ikke endring; du slipper å gjøre det alene.
- «Jeg vet jo dette allerede.» → Du trenger ikke mer å forstå, du trenger en oppskrift og trening.
- «Hvis jeg slipper opp, mister jeg resultatene.» → Behold ambisjonene, slipp prisen.
- «Hva om hverdagen tar meg igjen?» → Det er et treningsprogram, ikke et kurs du ser ferdig.
- «Det er mye penger.» → Hva koster det å fortsette som før? + 12 × 1.490 kr / Klarna.
- «Jeg har ikke tid.» → 20–40 minutter i uka er nok til å komme i gang.

## Tone of voice

Ine sounds like a warm, honest coach who has been there herself and still trains every day. She is never a guru and never pushy.

1. **Varm og nær.** She writes as «jeg» to «du», by first name, and signs off «Klem» or «Varm hilsen, Ine». She invites replies and says «Jeg leser alle svar selv.»
2. **Ærlig og sårbar.** She shares her own story: dyslexia and still writing a bestseller, losing people close to her early, 2024 when «alt raknet». Vulnerability always ends in a lesson, never in self-pity.
3. **Rolig autoritet.** Her claims are research-based but plain. She mentions BI, 15+ years as a project leader, mental training, sport psychology, NLP and breathwork, then gets practical.
4. **Utfordrende, med omsorg.** She names the pattern and gently pushes: «Legg merke til den stemmen.» «Er det egentlig ro – eller er det kontroll?» She challenges the belief, never the person.
5. **Lekende.** Humor is one of her stated values. She uses analogies («80-tallsbil med tungt ratt til en Ferrari»), a light emoji, and admits «walk the talk – eller i hvert fall prøver så godt jeg kan.»

**The calm register wins.** The 2022 emails («Haster!», «Ikke gjør denne feilen», «Vil du ha dette?») were hype-driven. The 2026 voice is calmer and insight-led, and it fits the high-achiever reader better. Keep the 2026 register.

**Watch the templated voice.** The July 2026 webinar invites (Georgia-font template) read like an agency translation: «Denne onsdagen», «Det skjer noe annet». Her own drafts are more personal and concrete. Always edit agency or AI drafts back into her voice.

## Writing style

Her copy follows a repeatable arc: a scene she recognises, the hidden pattern, a reframe, proof, then one clear invitation.

**The arc behind most of her emails:**

1. **Åpne med gjenkjennelse.** A concrete scene or the reader's inner voice in «»: «Kanskje jeg ikke er laget for dette?», «Ved middagsbordet med familien, og halvparten av hodet er fortsatt på kundemailen.»
2. **Navngi mønsteret.** Control, high inner demands, moving the finish line, sitting alone with responsibility.
3. **Snu det.** «Innsikt er ikke det samme som endring.» «Dette er ikke et disiplinproblem.» «Du trenger ikke velge mellom driv og ro.»
4. **Bevis.** A client story with a name (Silje, Katrine, Anna, Hilde) or a number (the client who went from kitchen table to about 80 MNOK revenue), or her own story.
5. **Invitasjon.** One CTA, framed as a choice: «Er du en av oss?», «Jeg vil trene sammen med deg».
6. **P.S.** A reflective question or a reframe, not a second pitch.

**Formatting habits:**

- Short paragraphs, often one line. Many questions.
- Contrast built as «ikke X – men Y», with the en dash –.
- Lists with → for pains and ✓ for outcomes.
- Few emojis, placed with purpose: ❤️ 🚀 🧠 🌸 👉.
- In classic emails, one or two key lines in bold pink (#e843a3).
- Softeners such as «Kanskje…» and «Og vet du hva?» keep the challenge kind.
- Analogies from training and sport: mental training works like strength training, the five balls in front of goal, «mentaltrening er ferskvare».

**Word bank:**

| Bruk (her language) | Unngå |
| --- | --- |
| kapasitet, bærekraftige prestasjoner | «hustle», «gønne på», «bite tennene sammen» |
| tankekjør, hodet logger aldri av | generic «stress less» clichés |
| trene hodet, mental styrketrening | «tenk positivt», «riktig mindset» alone |
| spille deg god / spille andre gode | guru claims, «hemmeligheten» |
| din egen flaskehals, innsidejobben | shaming the reader for lacking discipline |
| overlevelsesmodus, kjøre med brekket på | false urgency («HASTER!»), fake countdowns |
| råskap, Du er rå | English buzzwords stacked; one per email at most (high achiever, gamechanger) |
| stå stødig, ro og klarhet | medical promises (curing burnout or anxiety) |

**Mechanics:** write in bokmål. Use «» quotes and Norwegian number format (14.900 kr). Use {{first\_name}} in the greeting. Always spell the program KAPASITETSKODEN™ the same way, and proofread: the home page has slips such as «på tide og rydde» and «trene eget hodet».

**Subject line patterns that work for her:**

- A personal question: «Kan jeg spørre deg om noe, {{first\_name}}?»
- A reframe of the pain: «Hva hvis mer ikke trenger å koste mer?»
- A pain named in the reader's words: «Overveldet – savner mestringsfølelsen?»
- A story teaser: «Jeg fikk meg en liten overraskelse på scenen»

Preview text should finish the thought, not repeat the subject.

**Email skeleton (value or sales):**

1. Hei {{first\_name}}, plus one line of context («Det er lenge siden sist», «For mange starter hverdagen igjen i morgen»).
2. A scene or question the reader recognises.
3. The pattern and the reframe.
4. One tool or story she can use today (SMART goals, the five balls, the week audit).
5. One CTA, or an ask to reply.
6. Sign-off, then a P.S. that adds a reflection or a book plug.

**Launch sequence (as built in this repo's `webinar-emails/`):**

1. Invites, 4 days before to the morning of the event: frihet, den indre stemmen, «sterk betyr ikke å tåle mer», «ikke sliten av det du tror», siste sjanse.
2. Day 1 after: thanks, recap and the four outcomes → offer and price.
3. Day 2: the high-achiever fear «Hvis jeg slipper litt opp…» + Kommunikasjonskraft bonus.
4. Day 2 alt: Silje's story (tvil i ny lederrolle).
5. Day 3: the brain prefers the familiar; live coaching.
6. Day 4: alone with responsibility; the kitchen table to 80 MNOK story.
7. Day 5: money; the cost of standing still; 12 × 1.490 kr and Klarna.
8. Close day: self-doubt («Hva om hverdagen tar meg igjen?»), Ine's own story, then «siste mail denne runden».
9. After close: graceful downsell to the book («Det er helt greit»).

**Cadence:** between launches, send one value email a week, using her «Mini-lifehack» format. The list went quiet for weeks at a time in 2026 (May, July and September), which weakens the next launch.

## Sales page and webinar playbook

The webinar page «Koster suksessen deg for mye?» is her strongest page; reuse its structure for every opt-in and sales page.

**Page order that works:**

1. **Headline = pain as a question:** «Koster suksessen deg for mye?» Subhead = the promise plus the guardrail: «…uten å senke ambisjonene for businessen din».
2. **«Er dette deg?»** A short, sensory description of her day («sliten selv når du hviler»), then what she still wants.
3. **What you learn:** 4 points, each with a «hvorfor» and a counter-intuitive reason («Det er ikke et stressproblem»).
4. **Who it is for:** a ✔ list ending with the guardrail (helse, energi, familieliv).
5. **Testimonials with a first name and a concrete result:** Anna («stå gjennom enhver gründerstorm»), Hilde («høyere lønn, bedre balanse hjemme»).
6. **Om Ine:** Norli bestseller list 2024, 15+ years in business, who she helps and how.
7. **FAQ** that removes friction: length, recording, preparation, «hva hvis jeg ikke er sikker?»
8. **Closing question** that hands the choice back: «hvor mye lenger vil du vente…»

**Flagship sales page rules:**

- Lead with capacity and the female-founder bottleneck, matching the page description.
- Present the value stack exactly once, then webinar price, ordinary price and payment plan.
- Call it a *treningsprogram*, not a course, and show the rhythm: a new module every other week, live coaching every other week, 20–40 min per week.
- Name the deadline and the bonus honestly; close the cart when you say you will.

**Webinar rules:** live only, about 90 minutes including Q&A, held on weekday or Sunday mornings (10:00–11:00). Every event page needs a current date. Say «Neste dato kommer» instead of leaving a past date up.

## Business strategy

The biggest wins are focus and rhythm: one flagship name, a fixed launch calendar, and a steady stream of leads from her talks between launches.

The list grew from 747 to 1.715 contacts between October 2025 and October 2026. Almost all of that growth came in launch months: December +118, January +228, March +223 and August +97. Quiet months added 0–27.

**Priorities, in order:**

1. **One flagship, one name.** «Meant 4 More», «Meant for More», DRIVE, Pure Mindset and Mindset Metoden all point at the same program. Sell only KAPASITETSKODEN™. Retire or redirect the Meant 4 More offers, and decide whether the community keeps its name as an alumni space.
2. **Get one source of truth for sales.** Since January 2025, Kajabi has recorded only about 13.300 kr across all offers, including 2 KAPASITETSKODEN™ payments totalling 100 kr. Real sales are clearly happening elsewhere (Klarna, invoice, Vipps). Until every sale is tracked, nobody can tell which launch or email worked.
3. **A fixed launch calendar: 3–4 webinar launches a year,** matching the seasons she already writes to: January (nytt år), March, mid-August («før hverdagen drar i gang») and late October («Hvor er du i desember?»). Plan the next one now; the last broadcast before this guide went out on 11 October.
4. **Turn every talk into leads.** At about two workshops a week plus talks, the stage is her best lead source. Copy the Strategisamling set-up from Kamille Weekend (a QR code to a 10-minute tool page, then a nurture email, then a webinar invite) for every event.
5. **Build the Mental Fitness Challenge as the year-round entry point.** Run the free beta now and collect replies and results. Then make it the standing lead magnet that ends in a KAPASITETSKODEN™ webinar invite.
6. **Make proof part of the program.** The April 2026 cohort kick-off went to 4 people. Interview every participant at weeks 6 and 12, and aim for three named stories with a concrete result per launch, like Silje's and Anna's.
7. **Keep 1:1 premium and scarce.** Keep it application-only and keep the scarcity honest («to ledige plasser»). Use KAPASITETSKODEN™ graduates as the 1:1 pipeline.
8. **Let the book feed the webinar.** Add a webinar CTA to every book resource page (Yoga Nidra, Visualisering, Livshjulet). Decide on a reprint or paperback now that the hardcover is close to sold out.

Avoid adding new program names or one-off products until the flagship sells predictably.
